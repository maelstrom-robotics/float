# MATE Floats! 2027: rules summary (RANGER)

Source: *2027 MATE Floats! Preview Mission, RANGER/PIONEER* (materovcompetition.org/2027). The official document wins if anything here disagrees.

## The dive (pages 1–6)
- Before the first dive, send at least **one data packet** to our receiver (5 pts).
- Each vertical profile: sink, **hold 2.0 m for 30 s**, sink to 4.0 m without touching the floor, then rise.
- Hold is proven by **7 packets in a row, 5 s apart, at 1.67–2.33 m**.
- On the way up, log readings at **4.0, 3.5, 3.0 ... 0.0 m (±20 cm)**: depth, temperature (within 2.0 °C), light.
- At the surface, send **at least 10 packets**.
- Do it twice. Buoyancy engine = 10 pts per profile (other method = 5).
- Graph **temperature vs depth** and **light vs depth** at the station (5 pts each).
- Touching the pool floor: −5 per profile. Total: 70 pts.
- Regional pools may use shallower targets: check with the regional coordinator.

## Data packet (page 4)
Company number, time, pressure (Pa/kPa) and/or depth (m/cm), temperature (°C), light (any unit we declare).
Example from MATE: `EX01 1:51:42 UTC 9.8 kpa 1.00 meters 23.6oC 0.546 V`.
Other teams transmit at the same time, so our receiver must show only our float.

## Build limits (pages 9–13)
- Under 60 cm tall (antenna included), under 18 cm wide. No tether, thrusters or cameras.
- Onboard batteries only: 12 V and 5 A max, one main fuse within 5 cm of the battery.
- NiMH or AGM batteries only. No alkaline, no LiPo.
- Pressure relief: pop-off end cap (O-ring seal ≥ 2.5 cm) or a store-bought valve set to ≤ 15 PSI (valve specs due May 1, 2027 or 2 weeks before our regional).
- DOC-004 paperwork (2 pages + 1-page wiring diagram) must describe how the float talks to the receiver and which sensors it uses.
